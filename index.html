Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I care for my wooden piece?

Hand wash and dry immediately — never soak or microwave. Re-oil every 4–6 weeks with food-safe mineral oil or our board butter to keep the wood conditioned.

Can I order a custom size or wood species?

Yes — most of our custom pieces start with the form at the bottom of this page. Tell us your dimensions, preferred wood and timeline and we'll follow up with options and pricing.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship throughout New Zealand. International shipping is available on request for select items — get in touch before ordering.

What if my piece has a natural imperfection?

Every board is one of a kind — small knots, mineral streaks and grain variation are part of the wood's story, not defects. We flag anything structural before it ships.
