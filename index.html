Behind the Grain

Inside the Valley Woods workshop... From the Workbench

Welcome! I'm the maker behind Valley Woods. My philosophy is simple: celebrate what mass production ignores. Industrial manufacturing treats timber imperfections as flaws, but I see them as a fingerprint of the natural world.

Every item in this collection is individually shaped in my workshop using local, sustainably sourced timber and rescued offcuts. By pairing traditional hand tools with clean, contemporary design, I forge lasting, practical wares that tell a story from the forest straight to your home.

Wood Care Guide

Solid timber lives and breathes. Follow these guidelines to ensure your items stay beautiful for a lifetime:

  • Hand Wash Only: Never submerge your boards in water or place them in a dishwasher. Wipe clean with mild soap and warm water.
  • Dry Instantly: Stand your boards upright on their edge to dry evenly on both sides. This prevents warping.
  • Oil Frequently: When the timber looks dry or dull, rub a generous layer of food-safe mineral oil or wood wax over the surface. Let sit for 20 minutes and buff off the excess.
